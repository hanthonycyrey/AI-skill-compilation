# After a negative outcome — structural reference

Confirm current review/appeal windows and fees for the specific authority via web search — these change and vary by body.

## First, diagnose why

Most negative outcomes trace back to one of:
1. **Insufficient personal/technical specificity** — especially for CDR/RPL-style reports; generic or team-framed ("we did...") writing doesn't demonstrate individual competency, and can also trigger plagiarism/AI-content flags.
2. **Occupation mismatch** — the actual duties described don't line up closely enough with the nominated ANZSCO code's task description.
3. **Insufficient qualifying employment or study** — doesn't meet the authority's minimum thresholds once properly assessed.
4. **Documentation problems** — missing certified translations, incomplete transcripts, missing a required component (e.g. EA requires all four CDR components present or won't proceed).

Ask the applicant for the actual outcome letter/reasons if they have it — don't guess at why without it.

## Realistic paths forward

- **Review/appeal within the authority** — usually has a specific time window and needs to substantively address the stated reasons, not just resubmit similar content. Only pursue this if there's genuinely stronger evidence or a clearer account to give — not as a way to "try again" with the same weak material.
- **Reapply with materially better evidence** — often the more realistic option if the gap was specificity/detail rather than a hard eligibility issue.
- **Reconsider the occupation code** — if the applicant's actual work genuinely fits an adjacent occupation better, that's worth exploring; don't shoehorn duties to fit a code they don't really match.
- **Additional qualification or bridging study** — sometimes the only real fix if the qualifying employment or study genuinely isn't there yet.

## What not to do

Don't help the applicant reword the same unsupported or generic claims to sound more convincing without adding real substance — assessing authorities that do human + automated review are specifically looking for exactly that pattern, and it's dishonest regardless of whether it's caught.
