# Master evidence checklist structure

Use this as the skeleton for the gap-analysis table in step 2 of the workflow. Not every category applies to every pathway — skip what's irrelevant (e.g. state nomination criteria don't apply to 189; sponsor/nomination categories don't apply to GSM).

## Categories

1. **Identity & civil status** — passport, birth certificate, name-change evidence if applicable, marriage/de facto/divorce documents if relevant to the claim or dependents.
2. **Nominated occupation & skills assessment** — assessment outcome letter, qualifications assessed, any conditions/limitations noted on the assessment (e.g. years of experience it recognises — must line up with EOI claims).
3. **English language** — test report, validity window check.
4. **Employment / points-relevant work experience** (per role claimed) — employment reference letter (duties, hours, dates, salary, on letterhead, signed by someone in authority — not just HR generic), payslips, tax/super records, contract, organisational chart showing the role's position if useful.
5. **Qualifications & study** — degree certificates, transcripts, Australian study evidence if claiming those points.
6. **Points-test-specific claims** — partner skills, professional year, community language accreditation (NAATI), STEM qualification points, regional study — each has its own specific evidence requirement, ask what's being claimed and check it against the current points table via search.
7. **Nomination-specific (190/491)** — evidence addressing the specific state/territory's published nomination criteria, not just the general visa criteria.
8. **Sponsorship-specific (482)** — employer's sponsorship approval status, nomination approval, contract, salary evidence, genuine-position evidence (see subclass-482.md).
9. **Health** — medical exam status/booking.
10. **Character** — police clearances for every country lived in 12+ months over the relevant look-back window, and honest disclosure of any prior visa refusals/cancellations or criminal history (never omit — non-disclosure is itself a PIC 4020 risk).
11. **Genuineness narrative** (where relevant — e.g. genuine temporary entrant considerations, genuine position for 482) — a coherent, specific personal/professional narrative, not generic boilerplate.
12. **Dependents/family members included** — their own identity, health, character, and (if relevant) relationship evidence.

## Checklist output format

Present as a table: Category | Status (Have / Partial / Missing) | Notes / quality issues | Next action | Rough time-to-obtain. Sort blockers (skills assessment, English, character issues) to the top since they gate everything else.
